RULES OF COMBAT

1. Standard Chess Rules

Every rule of orthodox chess applies: castling, en passant, pawn promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, and the 50-move rule.

2. Elemental Combat Feedback

Pieces do not deal health-point damage; captures are real chess captures accompanied by elemental spell animations and Web Audio soundscapes.

3. Controls

  • Mouse / Touch: Click or tap a piece to highlight legal moves (dots) and attack targets (rings). Click again to deselect or click destination to move.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys to navigate squares, Enter or Space to select / move, Escape to cancel.
  • Hotkeys: Z to Undo, F to Flip Board, R to Restart.

4. Combo System & Critical Finishers

Consecutive captures build an Elemental Combo streak with dramatic visual intensity without altering chess mechanics.
